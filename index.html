Our Philosophy

Intentional Sourcing & Slow Hearth Craft

We partner directly with smallholder coffee producers in Huila, Yirgacheffe, and Antigua, paying an average of 2.8× Fair Trade minimums. We roast twice weekly in our vintage cast-iron drum roaster to celebrate distinct terroir rather than roast level.

Our bakehouse wakes up at 4:00 AM. Using heritage stone-ground wheat from regional farms and a 10-year-old sourdough mother, our baked goods ferment for a full 48 hours for rich digestible flavor and irresistible crust.
